# ⛳ GolfOS

The modern open-source platform for golf tournaments, live scoring, leaderboards, player statistics and social rounds.

## Features

### Tournaments
- 🏌️ Tournament management
- 📱 Live scoring
- 📊 Live leaderboard
- 👥 Player management
- 📄 PDF results
- 📈 Statistics
- 🏌️‍♂️ Multi-course support (rating, slope, par per hole)
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Flight / group management
- 🏆 Season standings (Order of Merit)
- 🔔 Notifications

### Social Rounds
- 🤝 Invite friends for a round
- 📅 Propose a date/time and let friends vote/confirm
- ✅ RSVP (accept / decline)
- 💬 Comments on a round
